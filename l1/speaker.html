Speaker Notes — Lecture 1

AI & ML · BTech CSE Sem 7 · Introduction to AI & Intelligent Agents · ~60 min
① Hook: AI Today ② Chashma Story ③ What is Intelligence ④ Define AI ⑤ AI Around Us ⑥ Intelligent Agent ⑦ Agent or Not? ⑧ Exam Vocabulary ⑨ Wrap-Up
1

Opening Hook

5 min
Say this "Good morning everyone. Welcome to Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Before we begin — how many AI systems do you think you've already used today? Take 10 seconds. No right or wrong answer."

Take responses — expect ChatGPT, Google Maps, Instagram, Alexa.

Then ask "Did anyone use Gmail today? Google Search? YouTube? Amazon? Spotify?"
Land it "Congratulations — whether you realised it or not, you've already interacted with AI several times today. By the end of this lecture, you'll begin seeing AI everywhere."
2

Story — Finding the Spectacles

6 min
Say this "Let's imagine a situation. Your mother says — 'Beta, meri chashma bedroom mein kahin reh gayi hai. Zara dhoond ke laao.' Now tell me honestly — would you immediately start opening every drawer? Would you check the kitchen first?"

(Class laughs — "of course not")

Continue "Most of us would check near the pillow, the bedside table, the study table, near the laptop. Why? Because from experience, these are the places glasses usually turn up. Notice — you didn't KNOW where they were, yet you followed a strategy. You searched intelligently. Today we begin teaching machines to solve problems the same way. That journey is called Artificial Intelligence."
Teaching tipLet 2-3 students actually answer "where would you look first" before revealing the point — the debate makes the insight land harder than just telling them.
3

What Makes Someone Intelligent?

7 min
Say this "Before defining AI, let's understand something more basic — what IS intelligence? Who is the most intelligent student in your class? Now tell me WHY — not who, why."

Write responses on board as they come: learns quickly, solves problems, makes good decisions, thinks differently, learns from mistakes.

Build this mind-map live Intelligence / | \ Learning Reasoning Decision-Making \ | / Problem Solving | Adaptability
Land it "Notice something — nobody said 'because he has the biggest notebook' or 'because he memorises everything.' Knowledge and intelligence are not the same. A person is intelligent because of HOW they use knowledge, not how much they know."
Ask the classCold-call one student: "Name someone you think is intelligent, and tell me why in one sentence." Use their exact wording in the wrap-up line above — it lands better coming from a peer.
4

Defining Artificial Intelligence

8 min
Say this "Now we're ready. Artificial Intelligence simply means making machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. Notice — the definition never mentions robots. Never says ChatGPT. Never says humanoids. AI is not a machine. AI is an ability."
Write on boardInstead of memorising the definition, remember 5 abilities: LEARN · REASON · DECIDE · SOLVE PROBLEMS · ADAPT
Ask the class "So — can software be AI?" (Yes) "Can a mobile app be AI?" (Yes) "Can a robot exist WITHOUT AI?" (Yes) — "A robot without intelligence is just a machine."
Exam alert"Define AI. What are the 4 approaches (thinking humanly / thinking rationally / acting humanly / acting rationally)?" is a classic 2-mark. This course focuses on acting rationally — best possible action given available information, not literal human-like thinking.
5

AI Around Us

6 min
Say this "Let's see if AI is really everywhere. Take Google Maps — does it randomly suggest routes? No — it studies traffic, road closures, travel time, then decides. Gmail — how does it know which email is spam? Netflix — why don't all of us get the same recommendations? Because it has learned OUR preferences."
ApplicationIntelligent Task
Google MapsBest route

Land it "AI is not about displaying information. AI is about making intelligent decisions based on available information."
6

The Intelligent Agent

10 min
Say this "Let's understand one of the most important concepts in AI — the Intelligent Agent. Imagine a Swiggy delivery partner. He receives an order. Checks the location. Observes traffic. Chooses a route. Delivers the food. Throughout, he's continuously observing, deciding, acting."
Draw on boardEnvironment ↓ Perceive ↓ Think / Decide ↓ Take Action
Formal definition "An Intelligent Agent is anything that perceives its environment, makes decisions, and performs actions to achieve a goal. An AI system behaves exactly like the Swiggy rider — gather information → process → decide → act. This perceive → decide → act cycle is the heart of AI."
Common confusionStudents think an agent must "look" intelligent or human-like. Emphasise next section immediately — a thermostat qualifies, a calculator doesn't.
7

8

Exam-Ready Vocabulary

4 min
Say this "These are the concepts that frequently appear in university exams. Don't memorise lengthy definitions — understand the concept first. If you understand how Google Maps or Gmail works, you'll explain AI naturally in the exam."
  • Artificial Intelligence — the ability, not a machine
  • Intelligence vs Knowledge — using info vs having info
  • Intelligent Agent — perceive → decide → act
  • Perceive, Decision, Action — the 3-word cycle
Likely questionsDefine AI · Define Intelligent Agent · Give examples of Intelligent Agents · Explain AI with real-life applications
9

Wrap-Up & Transition

6 min
"An Intelligent Agent perceives, decides, and acts —
and AI is simply that ability, wherever it shows up."
Say this "Today answered: What is Artificial Intelligence? Next class answers something more interesting — suppose Google Maps has hundreds of possible roads. How does it choose the best one? Suppose a chess computer has 30 possible moves. Does it guess, or follow a strategy? That takes us into one of the most fundamental topics in AI — Search."
