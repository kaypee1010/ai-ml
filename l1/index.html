Before the definition — a story

The Chashma That Started It All 👓

🗣️ "Beta, meri chashma bedroom mein kahin reh gayi hai. Zara dhoond ke laao."

You didn't randomly open every drawer. You didn't check the kitchen. You went straight for the pillow, the bedside table, the study table — because experience told you where to look.

That's it. That's the seed of Artificial Intelligence — searching intelligently, using what you know, instead of guessing randomly. Machines that do this are what we spend this whole course building.

Key insight

You didn't know where the glasses were — yet you followed a strategy. Intelligence is about the strategy, not certainty.

Nobody is intelligent because of a "big notebook." It's about how you use what you know — not how much you know.

The formal definition (finally)

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Definition

Artificial Intelligence is the branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks requiring human intelligence — learning, reasoning, decision-making, problem-solving, and adaptation.

Notice what's missing from that definition: no robots, no ChatGPT, no humanoids. AI is not a machine — AI is an ability.

The single most important concept in this unit

The Intelligent Agent

Definition

An Intelligent Agent is anything that perceives its environment, makes decisions, and performs actions to achieve a goal.

Environment
↓
Perceive
↓
Decide
↓
Act

Think of a Swiggy delivery partner: receives the order → checks location → observes traffic → picks a route → delivers. This Perceive → Decide → Act loop is the heartbeat of every AI system.

Flashcards — tap to reveal

Perceive tap to flip
The agent gathering information from its environment through sensors — e.g. a thermostat sensing room temperature.
Decide tap to flip
Choosing the best action based on what was perceived — e.g. thermostat decides AC should switch ON.
Act tap to flip
Executing the decision through actuators — e.g. thermostat actually switches the AC relay.
Goal tap to flip
What the agent is trying to achieve — e.g. keep the room at 24°C, or deliver food as fast as possible.

What the examiner is actually looking for

Exam Prep — Lecture 1

2-Mark Questions

  • Define Artificial Intelligence.
  • Define an Intelligent Agent.
  • Give any two examples of Intelligent Agents.

5-Mark Questions

  • Explain the characteristics of Artificial Intelligence.
  • Explain Intelligent Agents with suitable examples.

10-Mark Questions

  • Define Artificial Intelligence and discuss its applications.
  • Explain the concept of Intelligent Agents with real-life examples, including the Perceive-Decide-Act cycle.
